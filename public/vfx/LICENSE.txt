Re-encoding applied to every file:

	ffmpeg -i <source> -vf scale=256:256:flags=lanczos -pix_fmt ya8 \
	       -compression_level 100 <out>

512 -> 256 px because no sprite in this app is ever drawn larger than about
180 CSS px, and grey+alpha (PNG colour type 4) rather than RGBA because every
texture in the pack is white and src/lib/vfx.ts tints at draw time — the three
colour channels carry no information. Together that takes the 13 textures from
~740 KB to ~280 KB.
